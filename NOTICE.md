# Provenance / publication status

This workspace derives from the recovered Neko Files 0.1.2 snapshot and subsequent maintenance
releases. The original MIT LICENSE is retained with full attribution.

Its parser and archive modules originally referenced Arale-file-reader, which carried an
automated, unreviewed GPL-3.0 declaration generated by an LLM. In November 2026, the author
confirmed that this declaration was accidental, imposed no copyleft restriction, and authorized
the work to be distributed under the standard MIT License.

Consequently, all components and contributions in this repository are licensed under the
MIT License, and the previous public distribution hold has been lifted.
Host-side additions remain in the GPL MaiBot fork.
